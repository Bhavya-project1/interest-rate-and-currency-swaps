# Interest-Rate and Currency Swap Modelling

An Excel-based project examining the cash-flow and valuation mechanics of interest-rate and currency swaps.

Interest-rate swaps commonly exchange fixed and floating interest payments in one currency. Currency swaps can involve cash flows in two currencies and may include exchanges of principal.

## Project Scope

The workbook provides the calculations and assumptions for the swap structures modelled. Review it for the exact instruments, payment schedules, rates, notionals, and valuation conventions.

Relevant concepts include:

- Fixed and floating cash flows
- Notional principal
- Payment and reset schedules
- Reference interest rates
- Discounting and present value
- Currency-denominated cash flows and exchange rates

## Repository Contents

- `Swaps  (1).xlsx` — spreadsheet model.

## How to Use

1. Download the workbook and open it in Microsoft Excel.
2. Identify instrument inputs and market assumptions.
3. Review the cash-flow schedule and formula dependencies.
4. Modify only designated input cells to examine alternative assumptions.
5. Recalculate and inspect cash-flow and valuation outputs.

## Modelling Considerations

Swap valuation depends on the term sheet and market conventions, including day-count basis, business-day adjustments, payment frequency, reset dates, discount and projection curves, collateral terms, and—in cross-currency transactions—FX and cross-currency basis assumptions.
